# Inbox

Put anything here. Articles, PDFs, transcripts, screenshots, voice notes, your own rough
notes, a spreadsheet. Any format, any subject. No naming rules and no folders to choose
between.

Then say "ingest this".

Everything after that is the agent's job: reading it, working out what it is about,
writing and updating pages, and moving the file out of here into `raw/`. Files in `raw/`
are never edited. They stay exactly as you left them.

Pictures go in `../assets/`, which Obsidian handles automatically.
