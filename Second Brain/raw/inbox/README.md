# Inbox — drop anything here

Articles, PDFs, transcripts, screenshots, voice notes, your own scribbled notes,
a spreadsheet. Any format, any topic. No naming rules, no folders to choose.

Then tell the agent **"ingest this."**

Everything after that is the agent's job: reading it, deciding what it's about, writing
and updating pages, and moving the file out of here into `raw/`. Files in `raw/` are
never edited — they stay exactly as you left them.

Images go in `../assets/` (Obsidian puts them there automatically).
