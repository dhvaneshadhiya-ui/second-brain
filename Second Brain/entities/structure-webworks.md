# Structure Webworks

A Toronto-based digital agency building websites, e-commerce, CRM, SEO, and automation
for small and mid-sized businesses — self-described as serving "trades, legal, medical,
and retail businesses ready to scale." Author of
[[how-to-build-a-second-brain-with-claude]].

**Filed under `seo` rather than `meta`** — the entity is a business operating in the
content-and-SEO space, and that is the neighbourhood it will matter in if it comes up
again. The reason it entered this wiki (authoring a knowledge-management video) is
captured by links, not by its domain.

## As a content-marketing specimen

More interesting here than the agency itself is the *mechanics* of the video, which is a
clean example of a pattern worth recognizing:

- **Ride a named idea early.** Published ~2 months after [[andrej-karpathy]]'s gist,
  explicitly borrowing its credibility ("Inspired by the LLM Wiki pattern by Andrej
  Karpathy") while the term still had search novelty.
- **Comment-gated lead magnet.** "Comment 'Prompt' below and I'll send you the exact
  prompt" — the deliverable is withheld from the video, converting engagement into a
  contact list and feeding the algorithm comment volume at the same time.
- **Demonstrate the outcome, withhold the mechanism.** The video shows the finished
  structure and the workflow, but never the prompt that produces it. The value is real
  and the gate is on the one asset that is trivially copyable.
- **Broad hashtag spray** across model-name tags (`claudefable5`, `claudeai2026`,
  `claudecode`) — capturing search traffic on model generations with no established
  content supply.

None of this is an endorsement of the tactic; it is a record of one that was executed
competently against a topic this wiki also covers.
